# Clara Whitcombe — Proof-of-Concept Mechanical Audit

**Status:** non-canon prototype. This is a rules and presentation test, not a locked player character.

## POC rules switches

- Pulp hero generation is used for characteristics and archetype.
- Victorian/Gaslight-era occupation and skill bases are used.
- Low-pulp option: **one** human-plausible Gaslight Talent.
- Low-pulp optional hit-point rule: `(CON + SIZ) / 10`, rather than full Pulp double HP.
- No psychic skills, supernatural talents, magic, or weird science.
- Pulp age modifiers are omitted.
- Cthulhu Mythos starts at 0.

## Talent

**Keen Vision:** bonus die to Spot Hidden. This was chosen because it can be explained as trained, exceptional human observation and does not breach the campaign's capability ceiling.

## Why this is useful as a POC

The build produces a character who is emphatically competent without being generically good at everything: brilliant and observant, technically skilled, socially serviceable, physically ordinary, and notably slow/awkward (DEX 35). The mechanics reinforce the fiction instead of replacing it.
