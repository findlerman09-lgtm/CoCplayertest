H3 — MERTON'S QUERY

Sir,

The former occupier has again asked whether a small locked case or parcel from the rear room was included among the miscellaneous effects removed on the 7th. I cannot identify it upon your account. Kindly ask the men whether it was placed with goods to store, treated as refuse, or left at the premises.

Yours,

C. H. Merton

In Samuel's pencil at the foot:

Ask Vale / Naylor what came from rear.
