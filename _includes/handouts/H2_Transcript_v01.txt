H2 — EXTRACT FROM PENFOLD'S DAY BOOK

DATE — CUSTOMER / ROUTE — ENTRY

2 Aug. — Mrs W. Prentice — Whitechapel Road to Stepney Green. Two rooms. Bedding & one chest to store. Van.

4 Aug. — R. L. Fenner — Aldgate to Bishopsgate. Office desk, chairs, four cases. Deliver same day.

7 Aug. — C. H. Merton, agent — Western Whitechapel premises. Residual shop & office effects. Selected goods to store; refuse as directed. Balance awaiting instruction. V./N.—ask re rear room.

9 Aug. — Mrs T. Havers — Commercial Road to Mile End. Bedroom furniture & boxes. One van.

11 Aug. — S. Dunning — Effects to warehouse. Wardrobe, bedstead, chairs, trunks. Lot 47.

27 Sept. — W. Canning for P. Griggs — Commercial Road premises. Remaining shop fittings & household effects. Part to store; trunks delivered.

29 Sept. — Mrs Ellery — Whitechapel Road rooms to St George’s. Two-room removal. Upper floor. V./N.

1 Oct. — M. Sayer — Two trunks & chest to store. Lot 52.
