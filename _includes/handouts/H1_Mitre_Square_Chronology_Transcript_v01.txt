H1 — THE MITRE SQUARE CHRONOLOGY

TIME — ESTABLISHED EVENT

About 1:00 a.m. — Catherine Eddowes is released from Bishopsgate Police Station.

1:30 a.m. — PC Edward Watkins passes through Mitre Square; no body is present.

About 1:35 a.m. — Joseph Lawende and his companions see a man with a woman believed, from her clothing, to have been Eddowes near Church Passage. Lawende doubts he could recognize the man again.

1:44 a.m. — Watkins discovers Eddowes’s body.

About 2:55 a.m. — PC Alfred Long finds a piece of Eddowes’s apron in Goulston Street. He says it was not there when he passed at about 2:20.

Established

The outer murder window is 1:30–1:44. The apron piece was later elsewhere.

Not established

Exact murder minute; offender’s route; who carried the cloth; exact deposit time; authorship of the graffito.
