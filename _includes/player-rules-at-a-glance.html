Player Rules at a Glance06

The Rippers uses ordinary Cthulhu by Gaslight investigators with a narrow selection of Pulp rules. There are no Pulp archetypes or +100 archetype points. Each investigator has exactly one human-scale Pulp talent.

Luck

Everyone begins Who is Jack? with Luck 55. There is no Luck Recovery roll before the first session. Luck Recovery begins at the start of subsequent sessions.

After an eligible skill or characteristic roll, you may spend Luck 1-for-1 to lower the result. You may spend no more than 25 Luck on one eligible roll.

Ordinary Luck spending cannot alter Luck rolls, damage rolls, Sanity rolls, Sanity-loss rolls, or pushed rolls. Criticals, fumbles, and firearm malfunctions are not bought away. Luck also cannot halve Sanity loss, cancel a weapon fumble, or cancel a firearm malfunction.

Luck Recovery after the first session

  • Roll higher than current Luck: recover 2D10+10 Luck.
  • Roll equal to or below current Luck: recover 1D10+5 Luck.
  • Luck cannot exceed 99.

It's Only a Scratch!

Enabled — 20 Luck. After taking damage, spend 20 Luck to recover 1D6 HP, not exceeding maximum HP. If the injury already inflicted a Major Wound, recovering HP this way does not remove that Major Wound.

Avoiding Unconsciousness

Enabled. When you would become unconscious, spend 1 Luck for the current round, then 2, 4, 8, 16… for successive rounds. The cost doubles each round. When you stop paying, you become unconscious.

Avoiding Certain Death

Disabled. There is no campaign rule allowing you to empty your Luck pool to reverse otherwise certain death.

These investigators remain entirely human. No talent grants psychic ability, magic, Weird Science, supernatural intuition, impossible equipment, or superhuman capability.
