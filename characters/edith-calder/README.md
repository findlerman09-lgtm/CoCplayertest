# Edith Calder — active replacement investigator

Dossier routes: overview, sheet, people, correspondence, documents, possessions and scenario at `/characters/edith-calder/`.

Edith Calder replaces Clara Mercer in the fourth active investigator slot after Clara's death during actual play. They had met as competing journalists earlier in Adventure I; the exchange is controlled by what occurred at the table.

Player state, unlock, items and knowledge are new. Clara's prior state is not imported. A newly issued PBKDF2 dossier verifier (not the plaintext password) is stored in `_data/characters.yml`. The retired Clara route is kept as an archival record, inaccessible through the current cabinet. The cropped sepia portrait is derived from the player-approved Edith Calder illustrated character sheet.
