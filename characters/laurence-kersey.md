background: >-
  Laurence Kersey is a London-born general medical practitioner, the son of chemist and druggist Alfred Kersey. He grew up around practical medicine before training at London Hospital Medical College and qualifying M.R.C.S. Eng.; L.S.A. Lond. He now combines paying general practice with bounded charitable dispensary duties in East London. Kersey solves problems by putting events back into causal order, refuses to call uncertainty knowledge, and is willing to use his own hands when somebody needs saving.
history: >-
  Kersey once treated a carman injured beside an overturned cart. Reconstructing the accounts showed that the man's collapse preceded the cart accident rather than simply resulting from it. The case taught Kersey that a collection of true facts can still produce a false explanation when they are placed in the wrong order. His lasting lesson is simple: an explanation can contain every true fact and still put them in the wrong order.
